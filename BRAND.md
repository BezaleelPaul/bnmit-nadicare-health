# SOMYTHRA — Brand and Product Charter

**Version:** 1.0  
**Identity transition date:** 18 November 2026  
**Previous working identity:** NadiCare

## 1. Brand definition

**SOMYTHRA** is the name of an evolving research and technology project focused on understanding the human body as an interconnected living system.

**Core statement:** The science of understanding the human body as an interconnected living system.

**Brand line:** Life is not a collection of parts. It is a system of relationships.

SOMYTHRA should feel like the name of an advanced biological research civilisation: precise, exploratory, intelligent, and quietly futuristic. It should not resemble a hospital, supplement, fitness brand, or diagnostic product.

## 2. Invented etymology

SOMYTHRA is a constructed project name. Its internal, invented components are:

| Element | Project-defined meaning |
| --- | --- |
| SOM | The living vessel — the human body |
| YTH | Interdependence — biological systems influencing one another |
| RA | Pattern intelligence — understanding relationships across those systems |

**Combined meaning:** the intelligence of understanding the interconnected living body.

This is a fictional brand etymology. It is not presented as a word or translation from a historical or living language.

## 3. Mission

Make physiological understanding more accessible, contextual, and useful for individuals and communities, without sacrificing scientific honesty, privacy, consent, or dignity.

## 4. Product architecture

### SOMYTHRA Core
The foundation for data ingestion, sensor metadata, signal-quality checks, personal baselines, and privacy-aware storage.

### SOMYTHRA Twin
An exploratory representation of an individual's changing physiological patterns. It must communicate uncertainty and avoid implying a complete or clinically validated model of a person.

### SOMYTHRA Field
Community-oriented workflows that may support wellbeing awareness, environmental strain awareness, and field research. It must not enable punitive worker or student surveillance.

### SOMYTHRA Research
Reproducible signal-processing experiments, dataset documentation, model evaluation, and validation workflows.

## 5. Principles and safeguards

1. **Separate evidence from interpretation.** Label raw measurements, derived features, estimates, model outputs, and hypotheses distinctly.
2. **Never hide poor data quality.** Missing, noisy, or implausible signals must be surfaced, not silently converted into confident conclusions.
3. **Do not overclaim.** No diagnosis, treatment recommendation, emergency prediction, or clinical-performance claim without the relevant evidence, validation, and regulatory basis.
4. **Respect consent and privacy.** Explain what is collected and why; minimise collection and retention.
5. **Design for accessibility.** Prefer affordable, offline-aware workflows where technically feasible.
6. **Protect people from surveillance misuse.** Community and workplace insights should be supportive, proportionate, and non-punitive.
7. **Validate hardware and models.** A sensor reading or software demo is not, by itself, evidence of physiological accuracy.

## 6. Communication rules

Prefer:
- physiological patterns
- signal quality
- personal baseline
- interconnected systems
- wellbeing awareness
- research prototype
- exploratory digital twin
- community-centred insights

Avoid unsupported claims such as:
- medical-grade
- clinically proven
- diagnoses conditions
- predicts emergencies
- detects disease
- guarantees safety

Clearly identify simulated data and distinguish it from measurements collected from physical sensors.

- `SOMYTHRA`: project identity

## 8. Brand maturity and clearance

The name is a creative working identity, not a legally cleared mark. Before commercial or public launch, conduct independent checks for trademarks (including relevant software, device, and technology-service classes), domains, app stores, code-hosting namespaces, and social handles. Search-engine absence is not proof of availability.

## 9. Change control

This charter describes intended identity and direction. It does not certify that every module is implemented, validated, secure, or ready for deployment. Implementation status belongs in technical documentation, tests, and release notes.
